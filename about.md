<b>Who am I?</b>

<br>

<p align="justify">I’m a digital data analyst. I’ve worked across three quite different worlds — e-commerce, banking, and now television and the environment of an SVOD streaming platform — and the thing they have in common is that none of them can answer its most important questions without clean measurement underneath. That’s the part I enjoy: making sure the numbers people act on actually mean what they think they mean.</p>

<p align="justify">Day to day I work with BigQuery, dbt, Google Analytics, Looker and Tableau, and I like building things end to end — from extraction and data modelling to the dashboard someone finally looks at. What interests me most is the measurement layer itself: <b>user tracking</b>, <b>server-side measurement</b> and <b>identity stitching</b> — the problem of recognising that a person browsing on a phone in the morning and a person watching on a TV set in the evening are the same viewer. I also work a lot on measuring the <b>performance of social media and marketing channels</b>, where the hard part is rarely the reporting but deciding what deserves the credit.</p>

<br>

<b>Education</b>

<br>

<p align="justify">I studied <b>Sociology at Charles University</b> in Prague. That’s where the analytical groundwork comes from: research methods and the methodology of science, hypothesis testing, and mixed-method research design combining quantitative and qualitative data — mostly in SPSS. It’s also where I picked up the habit of asking how data came into existence before trusting what it says. During my studies I spent a semester at the <b>University of Oregon</b> and a year at the <b>University of Colorado Denver</b>, at the School of Public Affairs.</p>
